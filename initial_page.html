A Match-3 Puzzle Adventure

"I had a great fall... now it's YOUR turn to crack some eggs!"

— Humpty Dumpty, Your Egg-cellent Host PLAY NOW!

HOW TO

MATCH 3

Swap eggs to match 3 or more in a row. Pretty eggs-actly what you'd expect!

CRACK 'EM

Matched eggs crack and disappear. Don't worry, no chickens were harmed!

SCORE BIG

The more eggs you match, the more points! Aim to be an eggs-pert!
